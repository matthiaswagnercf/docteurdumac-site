Frequently asked questions

How much is the travel fee?

It depends on your city. I tell you in writing before the visit, along with the proposed time.

How soon can you come?

Say how urgent it is in the form: today, this week or no rush. I reply with a specific time.

Do I need to bring my Mac somewhere?

No, I come to your home or office. I diagnose the problem at your place. If I can fix it on site, I do. If not, I tell you what it will cost and take the device for the repair.

Do my photos and documents stay private?

At your place, I work in front of you. For remote help, you see everything I do. If I need to take the device, I tell you first.

How does remote help work?

The session starts only when you ask for it. You see everything I do on your screen, from start to finish.

What should I have ready for the visit?

The device’s charger, your computer password and the password for your Apple, Google or Microsoft account. Also note when the problem started. See how a visit goes.

Do you also handle PCs?

Yes. The Mac is my specialty, but I also look after Windows PCs.
